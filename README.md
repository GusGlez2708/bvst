# bvst

A fast-paced 2D side-scrolling shooter built with Flutter for Android and iOS.  
You play as a semi-organic android born from corrupted human memories, trapped inside a collapsing digital world. Enemies aren’t just monsters – they’re manifestations of raw emotions like Fear, Anger, Guilt, Oblivion, and Silence, each one with its own attack patterns, projectiles, and arena.

The game is designed around tight, readable combat: simple controls, clean cartoon visuals, and clear feedback so every hit, dodge, and shot feels intentional. The goal isn’t just to “beat the levels”, but to survive long enough to piece together who you were before you became code.

- 🎮 Horizontal fixed 2D gameplay (shooter / action)
- 👁️ Original universe and enemies themed around emotions
- 💥 Player and enemy projectiles with clear hit feedback
- ❤️ Heart-based life system for quick readability
- 📱 Built with Flutter, targeting Android & iOS
- 🔊 Custom music and SFX to match each area’s mood
