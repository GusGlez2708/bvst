# Echoes of Viridis

Echoes of Viridis is a challenging 2D arcade-style game with a pixel art aesthetic, developed for mobile devices. You play as R-013, a “Reconstruct” — a fragmented human consciousness awakened inside the digital ecosystem of Viridis, an artificial world created to preserve life after the collapse of Earth. Your goal is not to save the world, but to rebuild who you were before becoming code, facing the corrupted emotions that now rule the system.

From the bottom of the arena, you move left and right and shoot upward, while bosses appear at the top of the screen: manifestations of Fear, Anger, Silence, Guilt, and finally Conscience. Each one has its own attack patterns — projectiles, lingering flames, time-slowing effects, and ambushes — forcing you to react quickly, memorize patterns, and adapt in fast-paced, intense, and highly replayable runs.

# Key Features

🎮 2D arcade-style gameplay: lateral movement of the protagonist and vertical shooting at enemies moving left to right at the top of the arena.

👁️ Pixel art aesthetic: a semi-organic digital world, with characters and enemies designed in pixel art and tailored for mobile screens.

🧠 Enemies based on human emotions:

Fear: moves and bombards the player from above.

Anger: launches fast attacks and leaves digital fire on the ground that lingers and damages on contact.

Silence: distorts and slows the player, altering the rhythm of the battle.

Guilt: hides in the environment, attacks from the shadows and can only be damaged during brief vulnerability windows.

📖 Story mode through encounters: before and after each boss, dialogue scenes between R-013 and each “Echo” are displayed, with portraits and text boxes revealing fragments of Viridis’ story and the protagonist’s lost identity.

📱 Built with Flutter: the entire project is developed with Flutter, targeting mobile devices as part of a school project.
